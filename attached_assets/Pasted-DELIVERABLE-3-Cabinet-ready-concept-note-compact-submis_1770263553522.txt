DELIVERABLE 3 — Cabinet-ready concept note (compact, submission-ready)
Title

LiJOBS — Liberia Jobs Observatory & Labour Exchange System
A National Job Creation Data Platform for Real-Time, Trustworthy, Privacy-Safe Employment Statistics and Employment Services.

Sponsoring Institution

Ministry of Labour (MoL) in collaboration with LISGIS and key MDAs; implemented through a national steering committee and a technical program unit.

Problem Statement

Liberia cannot reliably answer, in near real-time, “How many jobs were created, where, by whom, for whom, and for how long?” across public and private sectors—especially in an economy with significant informal employment. This limits evidence-based policymaking, accountability for public investments, targeting of skills programs, and credibility with donors/investors.

Goal

Establish a single national platform that (1) measures and verifies job creation and (2) provides labour exchange services to reduce job-matching frictions, while protecting citizens’ privacy.

Objectives

Produce national, county, district, and sector dashboards on job creation, retention, and labour underutilization using recognised labour-stat concepts.

Deploy a digital labour exchange (job seeker registry, vacancies, matching, counselling, training provider directory) comparable to leading regional systems.

Introduce inclusive access via USSD/SMS and assisted service delivery for rural and low-connectivity populations.

Implement privacy-by-design governance (data minimization, purpose limitation, secure PII vault, audited access).

Scope (What the system will do)

Labour Exchange & Services: vacancies, applications, matching, employer onboarding, counselling appointments, training/certification linkages.

Jobs Observatory: employment spells + job creation events; verification workflows and trust scoring; county dashboards.

Institutional Workflows: Public Employment Centres (PEC) and county labour offices operate as assisted digital service points (like Nigeria’s exchanges).

Standards: occupational/industry coding aligned to ISCO-08 and ISIC Rev.4.

Governance

Steering Committee: MoL (chair), LISGIS, Ministry of Finance, CSA, key private-sector bodies, development partners.

Data Protection & Ethics: legal basis, consent management, data-sharing MoUs, independent oversight and audit.

Implementation approach (phased)

Phase 1 (MVP, 4–6 months): registry + vacancies + basic dashboards + USSD/SMS intake + core job creation reporting.

Phase 2 (6–12 months): verification engine + payroll/tax signals + PEC workflows + training provider marketplace.

Phase 3 (12–24 months): forecasting/AI insights, deeper integrations, open-data products, national scale-up.

Expected outcomes

Credible “jobs created” reporting for Cabinet and partners.

Reduced job search frictions and better placement outcomes.

Improved targeting of skills programs using labour-market intelligence.

DELIVERABLE 4 — Donor funding proposal (World Bank / AfDB / ILO style, condensed)
1. Project Summary

Project Name: LiJOBS — Liberia Jobs Observatory & Labour Exchange
Implementing Agency: Ministry of Labour (MoL)
Executing Partners: LISGIS; selected MDAs; telecoms; private sector bodies
Duration: 24 months (with 5-year sustainability plan)
Total Budget (indicative): USD 6.5M–9.0M (finalized after technical design & costing)

2. Development Objective

Increase employment outcomes and the credibility of job creation statistics by establishing an integrated national labour exchange and job creation observatory, aligned with international labour-stat standards.

3. Rationale / Evidence

Leading peers combine job matching + labour-market intelligence (Ghana GLMIS, Nigeria NELEX, Senegal SIME).

Inclusive access (USSD/call center) materially expands reach beyond smartphone users (Text4Jobs proof-of-concept).

4. Components and Activities
Component 1 — Digital Labour Exchange & Assisted Service Delivery (USD 2.0M–2.6M)

Job seeker registry, vacancies, applications, matching, counselling booking, employer services.

PEC/county-office workflows, training + SOPs, service quality monitoring.

Component 2 — Job Creation Observatory & Verification (USD 1.6M–2.4M)

Employment spells + job creation events; evidence capture; verification sampling; trust scoring; audits.

Public investment/donor project reporting module with geo-tagging to county.

Component 3 — Labour Market Intelligence, Standards, and Open Data (USD 1.0M–1.6M)

Standards alignment (ISCO-08 / ISIC Rev.4) and metadata.

Dashboards, policy briefs, skills demand insights; secure microdata access for authorized analysts.

Component 4 — Inclusion, Privacy, and Safeguards (USD 0.8M–1.2M)

USSD/SMS + call center integration (inclusive design).

Data protection governance (minimization, purpose limitation, audit logging).

Component 5 — Project Management, M&E, and Sustainability (USD 1.1M–1.2M)

PMU staffing, change management, procurement support, M&E framework, sustainability planning.

5. Results Framework (sample)

PDO Indicator 1: % of job creation records with Verified/Partial verification status.

PDO Indicator 2: # of placements facilitated through the platform (disaggregated by sex, age, county).

PDO Indicator 3: Time-to-publish national job creation dashboard (days after month-end).

Intermediate: # employers onboarded; # PEC staff trained; # USSD users; # training providers listed.

6. Implementation Arrangements

PMU housed at MoL; technical delivery via competitively procured systems integrator(s).

Data-sharing MoUs with LISGIS, payroll/tax systems (as feasible), and donor programs.

7. Procurement Plan (high-level)

Lot 1: Software platform build + cloud deployment + security hardening

Lot 2: USSD/SMS + call center integration

Lot 3: Change management + training + M&E

Lot 4: Independent security and data protection audit

8. Risks and Mitigation

Data quality: verification engine + sampling audits + employer trust scoring

Adoption: PEC workflows + incentives + employer onboarding support

Privacy: separated PII vault + strict RBAC + minimization + audit logs

Sustainability: staged O&M financing plan + capacity transfer + local hosting options
