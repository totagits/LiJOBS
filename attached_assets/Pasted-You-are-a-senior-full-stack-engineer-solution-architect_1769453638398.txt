──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2) PRODUCT VISION & NON-NEGOTIABLE PRINCIPLES
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Vision: A single national platform that provides real-time, trustworthy, privacy-safe job creation statistics for Liberia by tracking employment as time-bound “employment spells” (start/end), not vague headcounts.

Non-negotiables:
1) Job ≠ Person. A person can hold multiple jobs; a job can be filled by different people over time.
2) Track “employment spells” with lifecycle events (hire, separation, transfer, suspension).
3) Support ALL sectors: public, private, NGO/projects, informal, seasonal.
4) Multi-channel data capture: web, mobile (offline), USSD/SMS, bulk upload, and API integrations.
5) Verification & trust scoring: job claims must be verifiable via evidence and audits.
6) Privacy-by-design: separate PII or hash identifiers; show only aggregated public stats.
7) Strong audit trails and role-based access control.

4) KEY MODULES & FEATURES (BUILD ALL)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
A) National Job Registry (Core)
- Employers/Organizations registry:
  org types: PUBLIC_MDA, SOE, PRIVATE, NGO, DONOR_PROJECT, COOPERATIVE, INFORMAL_GROUP
- Establishments/worksites with GIS point mapping (PostGIS)
- Job positions (“job slots/roles”) and planned headcount
- Employment spells linking person ↔ job_position:
  - start_date, end_date, status (ACTIVE/ENDED/SUSPENDED)
  - contract_type (permanent/fixed-term/apprentice/intern/self-employed)
  - employment_type (full-time/part-time/seasonal/casual)
  - wage band (privacy-safe)
  - source_channel (HRMIS, employer portal, USSD, enumerator, API)
- Job events: hire, confirmation, promotion, transfer, separation, leave, suspension
- De-duplication: detect potential duplicate persons and merge with audit trail

B) Informal Economy & MSME Capture
- Self-report (worker or micro-business)
- Simplified job declarations with minimal fields
- Enumerator-assisted capture with offline-first mobile app
- Seasonal job tagging (agriculture/fisheries/construction surges)
- Verification workflows tailored for informal reporting:
  - community confirmation
  - site visit
  - signed lists/photo evidence
  - randomized audits

C) Bulk Upload + Data Validation
- Employer/MDA bulk upload (Excel/CSV templates downloadable)
- Server-side validation and error reports
- Partial acceptance: valid rows accepted, invalid flagged
- Ingestion batch tracking with statuses (RECEIVED/VALIDATING/ACCEPTED/PARTIAL/REJECTED)
- Data normalization rules + mapping to codes (occupation/sector/geography)

D) Verification & Trust Scoring Engine
- Verification case created per employment spell (or in batches)
- Methods: PAYROLL_MATCH, TAX_MATCH, PROJECT_DOC, SITE_VISIT, COMMUNITY_CONFIRM, RANDOM_AUDIT
- Evidence uploads: documents/photos/GPS check-in metadata
- Trust score 0–100 with configurable weighting:
  - payroll match = high
  - tax match = high
  - signed list + geo photo = medium
  - self-report only = low
- Verification status: PENDING/VERIFIED/REJECTED/NEEDS_REVIEW
- Risk-based queue (prioritize high-impact employers, anomalies, rapid spikes)

E) Analytics & Dashboards (Policy-ready)
Dashboards must support:
- National, county, district, and establishment views
- Jobs created over time (daily/weekly/monthly/quarterly)
- Net jobs (hires minus separations)
- Disaggregation: gender, youth (age band), sector, occupation, job type, formal vs informal
- Job sustainability: duration distribution, seasonal patterns, retention
- Project job impacts: jobs by project, cost-per-job placeholders (if budget data supplied)
- Verification quality: % verified, average trust score, records pending review
- Heat maps and GIS layers (PostGIS + map component)
- Export to PDF/Excel + scheduled report generation

F) Public Transparency Portal (Privacy-safe)
- Publish aggregated stats only (no PII)
- Open data downloads at aggregated level (county/sector/time)
- Clear methodology page (job definitions, verification model)
- Accessibility compliant UI

G) Audit Log & Compliance
- Immutable audit log for all create/update/delete/login/export/verification actions
- Evidence chain: who uploaded what and when
- Tamper-resistant approach (append-only logs; optional hashing)

H) Integrations (API-first)
Build connectors (stub adapters if real systems unavailable):
- CSA HRMIS/Payroll (read-only import)
- Liberia Business Registry employer identity (read-only lookup)
- Optional tax/revenue verification (permissioned)
- Donor project systems (generic API import)
All integrations must use:
- API keys + OAuth2 client credentials
- Rate limiting, retries, idempotency keys
- Dead-letter queue for failed ingestion

I) Offline-first Mobile App (Enumerator/County Officer)
- Capture organizations, establishments, people (hashed), job positions, employment spells
- Offline local database (SQLite)
- Sync engine (conflict resolution rules)
- GPS capture and timestamp for audit visits and evidence

J) USSD/SMS Capture (Rural-friendly)
- Minimal flows:
  - Worker self-registration (optional)
  - Job declaration (short)
  - Verification callback / status
- SMS parsing + short codes (simulate if no telecom access)
