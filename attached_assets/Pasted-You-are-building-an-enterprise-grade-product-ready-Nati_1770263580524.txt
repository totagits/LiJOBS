You are building an enterprise-grade, product-ready National Job Creation Data Platform for the Republic of Liberia called “LiJOBS — Liberia Jobs Observatory & Labour Exchange System”.

MISSION
Build a secure, privacy-safe, real-time platform that:
(1) Collects and verifies job creation data nationwide (public + private sectors) using “employment spells” and a verification/trust-score engine; and
(2) Operates a modern labour exchange (job seekers, vacancies, matching, counselling, training providers) with inclusive USSD/SMS access.

BENCHMARK FEATURES (must match)
- Ghana GLMIS-style: Job seeker registration, jobs browsing, counselling booking, training providers directory, employer job posting, and labour market statistics dashboards.
- Nigeria NELEX-style: job seekers + employers meet online; resume search; placement support; labour market info, with public employment centre workflows.
- Senegal ANPEJ/SIME-style: job offers portal; services for job seekers (orientation, training, entrepreneurship/project support, migration interest) and employer services; issue a jobseeker ID card/QR.
- Include inclusive access via USSD/SMS + call-center assisted enrollment (Text4Jobs concept).

LIBERIA “#1” DIFFERENTIATORS (must add)
- National Job Creation Observatory:
  - Model jobs as EMPLOYMENT SPELLS (start/end, employer, county, occupation, industry, wage band, hours).
  - Record JOB CREATION EVENTS from multiple sources (employer reports, project reports, payroll/tax signals, surveys).
  - Verification workflow + TRUST SCORE per record and per employer.
  - Evidence capture (documents/photos) with secure storage links.
- Privacy-by-design:
  - Separate PII vault schema; analytics uses non-identifying IDs.
  - Strong RBAC/ABAC, full audit logs, encryption at rest and in transit.
- County dashboards (15 counties) with maps; show job counts and percent share by county, sector, sex, youth, disability, etc.
- Offline-first mobile data capture for county/PEC officers (sync when online).
- Open APIs + partner portal for donor projects and large employers.

TECH STACK (implement exactly)
- Frontend: Next.js (TypeScript) + Tailwind + component library; responsive UI.
- Backend: Node.js (NestJS) + PostgreSQL; Prisma ORM.
- Auth: OAuth2.1 / OpenID Connect (or built-in auth for MVP) + 2FA; RBAC.
- Infra: Docker-compose for local; cloud-ready (Kubernetes manifests optional).
- Messaging: Redis + BullMQ (or RabbitMQ) for background jobs (verification, ETL, notifications).
- Storage: S3-compatible object storage for evidence files.
- Analytics: Postgres read replicas + dbt-style transforms OR a small warehouse schema; dashboards rendered in-app.

CORE MODULES (build all)
1) Identity & Access: users, roles, permissions; organization accounts (MoL admin, LISGIS analyst, employer admin, PEC staff, donor viewer).
2) Registry: employers, establishments, public employment centres, private employment agencies; geocoding to county/district.
3) Labour Exchange:
   - Job seeker profile + CV builder + skills tagging
   - Vacancy posting + application workflow + messaging
   - Matching service (rules-based MVP; ML-ready interface later)
   - Counselling appointments
4) Skills & Training:
   - Training providers, courses, enrollments, certifications
5) Jobs Observatory:
   - employment_spells, job_creation_events, job_source
   - verification_status + trust_score; employer trust snapshots
6) USSD/SMS:
   - USSD menus for “I’m looking for a job” / “I’m hiring”
   - SMS notifications (application status, job matches)
   - Call-center console to complete registrations on behalf of users
7) Dashboards:
   - Real-time national and county dashboards (jobs created, net jobs, retention, placements, vacancies, in-demand occupations)
   - Export to CSV/PDF; scheduled monthly Cabinet report pack
8) Audit & Compliance:
   - immutable audit.event_log, admin activity trails, data access logs
